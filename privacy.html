Privacy Policy — Yogesh Content Publisher

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Yogesh Content Publisher ("the app") is a private automation tool operated by Yogesh Dhandiya. It is used only by Yogesh to publish his own videos to his own accounts.

Google user data the app accesses

When Yogesh signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos to his YouTube channel (YouTube Data API). It uses this permission only to upload the videos, titles and descriptions that Yogesh has scheduled, and to read back the upload result.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored only on Yogesh's own computer, inside his self-hosted automation software. They are not uploaded to any other server.
  • The app does not collect, sell, rent or share any Google user data with anyone.
  • The app does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • The app does not access data belonging to anyone other than Yogesh.

Compliance

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Stored tokens can be deleted from the automation software at the same time.
