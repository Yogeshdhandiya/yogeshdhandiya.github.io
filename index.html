Yogesh Content Publisher

Yogesh Content Publisher is a private automation tool run by Yogesh Dhandiya. It publishes Yogesh's own short videos to his own social media accounts on a schedule, including uploading Shorts to his own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Reads a content calendar that Yogesh maintains.
  • Uploads the scheduled video, title and description to Yogesh's YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Does the same for his other social accounts.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only Yogesh signs in to it, and it only acts on his own accounts.
